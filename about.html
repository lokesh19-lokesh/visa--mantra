Who We Are

India's Trusted Career Placement Partner

Headquartered in Hyderabad, The Visa Mantra is a premier, ISO-certified career recruitment agency specializing in placing qualified and hardworking professionals in Europe's most vibrant economies (France, Slovakia, Italy, Poland, Netherlands, Denmark) and top Asian hubs (Malaysia, Singapore).

Since our inception, our focus has always been in providing safe, secure, and fully legal blue-collar opportunities. We take immense pride in mapping high-quality direct labor options that serve as the springboard for long-term career growth, offering stable income and a secure future for thousands of Indian families.

We act as the direct liaison between blue-collar candidates and foreign employers in major industrial channels such as Hotels, Restaurants, Warehouses, Logistics, Supermarkets, and Factories.
